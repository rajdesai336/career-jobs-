# CareerPro - Job Portal & Complete Placement Preparation Platform

An all-in-one web application built to help engineering and tech students prepare for placements and secure top tech roles.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 💼 Job Portal
- **25+ Curated Job Listings**: Real opportunities across Product Giants (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Flipkart, Atlassian), High-Growth Unicorns (Razorpay, Swiggy, Zomato), and Top MNCs (TCS, Infosys, Accenture).
- **Multi-parameter Filtering**: Filter by Category (Frontend, Backend, Full Stack, Cloud/DevOps, AI/ML), Experience Level (Fresher, 1-3 Yrs, 3+ Yrs), Job Type (Full-time, Remote), or instant search by keywords.
- **Application & Tracker**: One-click quick apply modal with form validation, real-time application tracker, and local bookmarking.
- **Post a Job Modal**: Employer simulation allowing recruiters to publish new tech roles dynamically.

---

### 2. 📚 Complete Preparation Module
Organized across 4 placement pillars:
1. **Aptitude**:
   - Quantitative Aptitude (Time & Work, Speed Distance Time, Profit & Loss, Percentages, Permutations & Combinations).
   - Logical Reasoning (Syllogisms, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding).
   - Verbal Ability & English (Subject-Verb agreement, Sentence correction).
   - Cheatsheets with formulas, shortcuts, and **interactive MCQ practice problems** with instant correctness feedback, score tracking, and detailed step-by-step explanations.
2. **DSA Core Foundation**:
   - Asymptotic Analysis & Big-O Complexity Cheatsheet ($O(1)$ to $O(n!)$) with data structure operation matrix.
   - Top 7 Algorithmic Patterns (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Fast & Slow Pointers, Modified Binary Search, DFS/BFS, Dynamic Programming, Monotonic Stack).
3. **Technical CS Fundamentals**:
   - Deep-dive revision notes and top interview Q&As for **Operating Systems**, **DBMS & SQL**, **Computer Networks**, and **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) & SOLID principles**.
4. **HR & Behavioral Round Preparation**:
   - Interactive breakdown of the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
   - Top frequently asked HR questions with "Why interviewers ask", "Winning Formula", high-scoring sample answers, and key Dos & Don'ts.

---

### 3. 🏢 Companies Hiring Process
Detailed profiles for top recruiting companies (**TCS**, **Infosys**, **Amazon**, **Google**, **Microsoft**, **Accenture**, **Wipro**, **Cognizant**):
- **Role & CTC Tiers**: e.g., TCS Ninja (₹3.36 LPA), Digital (₹7.5 LPA), Prime (₹11.5 LPA); Amazon SDE-1 (₹30-45 LPA); Google L3 (₹35-55 LPA).
- **Eligibility Criteria**: Academic cutoff percentages, eligible engineering branches, backlog policy, and gap year rules.
- **Selection Process & Rounds**: Round 1 (Online Assessment) through Technical, Techno-Managerial, and HR rounds.
- **Exam Pattern**: Platform, duration, question count per section, sectional time limits, and negative marking rules.
- **Syllabus & Proven Prep Strategies**: Exact topics tested in online tests and candidate tips.

---

### 4. 🧠 Dedicated Topic-Wise DSA Sheet
A structured SDE preparation sheet modeled after Striver's SDE Sheet and NeetCode:
- **Categorized by 10 Core Topics**: Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers & Sliding Window, Linked List, Stack & Queue, Binary Search, Trees & BST, Graphs, Dynamic Programming, Heaps & Greedy.
- **Problem Details**: Difficulty badge (Easy / Medium / Hard), Company tags (Google, Amazon, Meta, etc.), and direct link to practice.
- **Interactive Checkbox & Progress Tracker**: Real-time progress bar calculation and completion percentage badge.
- **Multi-Language Solutions**: High-performance solutions in **C++**, **Java**, **Python**, and **JavaScript** with code copying.
- **Personal Notes**: Integrated note-taking per problem saved in LocalStorage.
